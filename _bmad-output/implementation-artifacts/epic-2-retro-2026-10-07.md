### P2. First-pass boundary correctness and repair completeness need improvement

The story reviews found concrete defects, not a quota of hypothetical enhancements:

- **2.1 R1:** new shell links bypassed recovery-code acknowledgement/history replacement. The first repair registered its
  guard inside the lazy route and still permitted departure before that route loaded. The second repair moved the refusal
  above the lazy boundary. This is a real first-pass and first-repair completeness issue.
- **2.1 R2:** the focused skip link stayed clipped/behind the sidebar. A focus assertion did not prove visual availability.
  Browser geometry/hit-testing caught the defect and discriminated the repair.
- **2.2 R1:** generic chunk recovery discarded logout-specific valid-cookie/local-hiding/close-browser guidance and offered
  an inappropriate in-app Reload action. The repair reused one eager logout-recovery owner rather than creating a new policy.

Primary sources: `tmp/agent-execution/2-1-basic-capability-driven-navigation/fix-report.md:10–11,34–46`, Story 2.1 spec
Deviations/Progress, and `tmp/agent-execution/2-2-working-routes-and-parent-navigation/fix-report.md:10–22`; committed fixes
in `d1be39a` and `cc135de`. Reviewer sessions `ses_eee72b5e1ffeBEf56fzp8klvxp` and `ses_eeb0334cbffe6XF3R8nJuKGhdL`.

The common pattern is **a shared control's interaction with an existing exceptional state**, not failure to implement the
ordinary UI. Default-success behavior during lazy loading and reuse of generic recovery across security states were the
weak points. This supports a targeted quality improvement, not a blanket claim that all implementation was poor.

Disposition: completed defects **accepted as repaired**. Prevention **fix now, proposed**: before handoff, trace the changed
shared control through its authorized, denied, loading, hidden/expired, ambiguous and departure states, and test the concrete
high-risk interaction. For these stories that means "shell exists before lazy owner", "logout chunk missing", and "focused
control actually visible". Reuse this boundary inventory during repair so fixing the steady state does not miss loading.
